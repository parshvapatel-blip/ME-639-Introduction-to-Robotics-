Plain English
Think of this like a globe. No matter how you tilt it, there is always exactly one straight line through the center — the Euler axis — that you can spin the whole globe around, in a single move, to bring it back to any target orientation. Drag the slider and watch the body frame swing home around that one yellow line.
Plain English
Think of an airplane: Yaw steers left/right (nose swings around the vertical axis), Pitch tilts the nose up/down, and Roll tilts the wings side to side. Undoing these three rotations in order — yaw, then pitch, then roll — walks the plane back to level, home orientation. Order matters: doing them in a different sequence lands you somewhere else!
